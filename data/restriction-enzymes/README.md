# Offline restriction-enzyme catalog — 17 October 2026

The app contains **407 distinct enzyme names**, with **377 modeled double-strand cleavage patterns**, **30 recognition-only entries**, and **18 Golden Gate sequence-design choices**. Vendor formulations such as HF, v2 and FastDigest are aliases/products of the base enzyme; they do not create extra cut sites. Isoschizomers with distinct enzyme names remain separate entries because they are separate products and may require different reaction conditions.

## Coverage and sources

`vendor-facts.json` contains factual recognition/cut notation and product-name extracts, not vendor descriptions or proprietary annotation databases. Its source keys identify these public primary references:

- `neb`: [NEB alphabetized recognition specificities](https://www.neb.com/en-us/tools-and-resources/selection-charts/alphabetized-list-of-recognition-specificities), 266 product/name occurrences, including nickases and homing enzymes. Formulation suffixes are normalized for searching.
- `neb-epigenetics`: NEB's [FspEI](https://www.neb.com/en-sg/products/r0662-fspei), [LpnPI](https://www.neb.com/en-sg/products/r0663-lpnpi) and [methylation-dependent enzyme information](https://www.neb-online.fr/en/epigenetics/restriction-enzymes-for-epigenetics/). These supplement the alphabetized chart. McrBC's `RC` is only a potential methylated recognition half-site, not a predicted complete cleavage site.
- `thermo-conventional`: [Thermo Fisher conventional selection tool](https://www.thermofisher.com/us/en/home/brands/thermo-scientific/molecular-biology/thermo-scientific-restriction-modifying-enzymes/restriction-enzymes-thermo-scientific/conventional-restriction-enzymes-thermo-scientific.html), all 191 listed enzyme cards.
- `thermo-fastdigest`: [Thermo Fisher FastDigest selection tool](https://www.thermofisher.com/us/en/home/brands/thermo-scientific/molecular-biology/thermo-scientific-restriction-modifying-enzymes/restriction-enzymes-thermo-scientific/fastdigest-thermo-scientific/fastdigest-enzyme-selection-tool.html), all 176 distinct FastDigest names, including 10 base enzymes absent from the conventional cards. Package sizes are not separate specificities.

These are checked table snapshots, not a live stock list or a claim that every regional/reseller/legacy SKU has been audited. "Fisher" here means the Thermo Scientific conventional/FastDigest enzyme ranges; Fisher Scientific also resells other manufacturers. New products and changed formulations require a catalog update. No account or network connection is needed to use this catalog.

## Cleavage and assembly limits

Offsets are zero-based strand cut boundaries measured from the start of the forward recognition motif. The scanner handles IUPAC sites, reverse orientation, downstream/upstream cut offsets, and circular-origin crossings. Linear sites whose required double-strand cuts fall outside the molecule are excluded from cleavage results. Ambiguous input bases must guarantee recognition; uncertainty is not silently resolved.

Nickases, two-sided/variable cleavage, methylation-dependent recognition (including DpnI), and EcoP15I's paired-site context are listed but cannot be selected for ideal digest or cloning prediction. Potential recognition counts for these entries do not establish actual enzyme activity. Other modeled entries still assume an ideal complete digest, without methylation, buffer compatibility, reaction kinetics or star activity. Near/interleaved cuts can remain unsuitable for physical-fragment assembly and are rejected by that workflow.

**PspFI** is recognition-only: the FastDigest chart depicts `CCCAGC` cuts at 5/1, while REBASE 404/Biopython gives 1/5. The app does not pick one silently. **BseMII** uses `CTCAG(10/8)`, confirmed by the [FastDigest product manual](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/MAN0012555_FastDigest_BseMII_UG.pdf); its selection-tool diagram incorrectly shows BseMI's specificity. **TspRI/TscAI** use equivalent alternative representations with/without explicit two-base flanks. NEB's unmarked BsmBI-v2 and BstZ17I chart rows are completed using the documented isoschizomer/cut geometry. The I-SceI conventional chart's concatenated strand diagram is normalized to its explicit NEB recognition/cut notation.

Golden Gate selectors support AarI, BbsI, BcoDI, BfuAI, BpiI, BsaI, BsmAI, BsmBI, BspMI, BspQI, BveI, Eam1104I, EarI, Eco31I, Esp3I, LguI, PaqCI and SapI. These create modeled 3/4-base 5′ ends. Other Type IIS enzymes are not automatically suitable for the current designer. Each choice passes the actual PCR/digest/ligation simulation. Temperature, ligase, buffer and activation requirements remain product-specific; **AarI/PaqCI require activator oligonucleotides**. See [NEB's PaqCI guidance](https://www.neb.com/tools-and-resources/usage-guidelines/usage-guidelines-for-golden-gate-assembly-with-paqci) and the relevant supplier manual. No universal cycling recipe or experimentally validated assembly success is claimed.

## Rebuild and verification

Run `python3 scripts/build-enzyme-catalog.py` from the repository root to regenerate `viewer/enzyme-catalog.mjs` from the checked facts. Python is only a developer tool; it is not an app dependency. `scripts/extract-enzyme-vendors.py SOURCE_DIRECTORY OUTPUT_JSON` documents the extraction, including the explicit diagram normalizations and 10 additional FastDigest specificities. It accepts `neb-web.txt` (the numbered, citation-bearing text view of the NEB table), `thermo.html`, and `fastdigest.html`. Raw vendor pages are not bundled. Re-extraction must be reviewed; the parser intentionally rejects conflicting definitions during build.

The unit suite verifies coverage of every extracted product, aliases, all modeled forward/reverse/circular cut geometries, special-enzyme rejection, and all 18 Golden Gate designs. The independent factual offset reference in `tests/fixtures/enzyme-reference.json` covers 372 entries from **Biopython 1.86 / REBASE EMBOSS version 404 (2024)**, with TspRI's equivalent flank representation accounted for.
